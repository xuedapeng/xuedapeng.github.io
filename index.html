BASICS · START FROM ZERO

Your farm, in your pocket

Account, devices, farms, messages and settings — five steps, and anyone can take over an irrigation system within ten minutes.

01

Account & Security

Sign up, log in, edit your nickname and password, recover access, switch accounts or sign out — every step is clear and controllable.

02

Device Registration & Management

Register devices by scanning or manually, and manage the device list, online status and core parameters in one place.

03

Remote Device Control

Switch pumps on and off, adjust valve openings, read live data — remote operation feels like standing in the field.

04

Farm Creation & Binding

Create a separate farm for each plot, bind devices to the right station, and keep the relationships clear at a glance.

05

Notifications · Reminders · Alerts

Irrigation completion notices, scheduled task reminders and device fault alerts — no important event goes missing.

06

Batch Control & Shortcuts

Select multiple devices and run them in one batch; clear the search box in one tap; reach frequent actions without detours.

ADVANCED · LET THE SYSTEM WORK

Set the rules. It handles the rest.

Task automation, rotation scheduling, team collaboration and LoRa networking — upgrade from "remote control" to "no babysitting needed".

Task Automation

Set triggers by time, humidity, water level and more. Irrigation runs automatically, with no one watching.

Rotation Scheduling

Zones irrigate in turn by time slot, spreading pressure and water volume evenly and keeping pumps off overload.

Farm Members & Transfer

Invite team members to manage the farm together with assigned permissions; hand over ownership in one tap when needed.

Location Management

Record farm and station coordinates, show device distribution on a map, and plan inspection routes more clearly.

Share · Transfer · Deactivate

Share a device temporarily, transfer it to another account, or deactivate retired equipment at any time.

LoRa Network Setup

Register LoRa devices and bind gateways, so remote plots without network coverage still stay connected.
